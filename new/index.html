About CyberSchool Academy

CyberSchool Academy is India's premier institute for cybersecurity education, founded in 2020 by a team of ethical hackers, legal experts, and digital forensics specialists. We operate at the intersection of offensive security, cyber law, and intelligence — producing India's next generation of cyber defenders.

Our curriculum is designed in collaboration with leading corporations, government bodies, and international certification boards (CEH, OSCP, CISSP). Every course features real-world labs, mentorship, and guaranteed placement support.
